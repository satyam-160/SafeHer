      How it works

      1. 1

        Press

        Hold the physical SOS button for 3 seconds. No unlock, no menu, no typing — works from a pocket or a bag.

      2. 2

        Locate

        ESP32 acquires a GPS fix and falls back to the last known location if satellites are lost.

      3. 3

        Alert

        A single SMS with a live maps link is dispatched to all 3 trusted contacts over the cellular network.

      4. 4

        Call

        The device auto-dials the contacts in priority order until one of them answers.

      5. 5

        Alarm

        A 125 dB siren runs to attract attention nearby. Hold 5s to send “I am safe” and stop it.

      Hardware (planned build)

      This page is a software simulation of the planned device. The hardware build is the next phase — BOM, firmware and the PCB layout are the follow-up deliverable.

      ESP32-S3Dual-core MCU with BLE & Wi-Fi — runs the whole state machine
      GPS NEO-6MStandalone location fix, stores last known position in flash
      4G SIM moduleLTE modem for SMS + auto-dial on the cellular network
      INMP441I²S microphone — records ambient audio as evidence
      MAX98357AClass-D amplifier driving the siren speaker
      0.96″ OLEDSSD1306 display — shows GPS, SIM, SOS and battery status
      Li-ion + TP4056Rechargeable cell with charge/protection IC and fuel gauge
      Big SOS buttonIP54 tactile switch, usable blindly — the only interaction needed
